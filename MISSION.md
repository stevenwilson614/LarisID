# LarisID — Why We Exist

**Read this before you ship code, copy, pricing, or policy.**  
If something you are building would violate this document, **stop and raise a red flag** — with the team, with Steven, or in your PR/issue. That applies to humans and to AI assistants working on this repo.

---

## What

**LarisID is a tool to help Indonesians start e-commerce businesses that last** — businesses that can supply enough income for a good life, not just a quick experiment that fades.

We are not building hype, gatekeeping, or “winner takes all” mechanics. We are building durable entrepreneurship.

---

## How

We help through:

1. **Affordable, accurate, timely data** — market and product intelligence people can actually trust and afford.
2. **Brilliant interpretations of that data** — clear guidance so sellers know what to do next, not just what the numbers say.

Data without judgment is noise. Judgment without honest data is manipulation. We need both.

---

## Why

This exists because there is a real desire to **create something big and impactful**. Helping entrepreneurs rings true at a personal level — building businesses is hard, and tools that respect sellers matter.

Profit for the project matters, but **purpose comes first**. Growth that betrays the mission is not success.

## Non-negotiables

These are not slogans. They are constraints on product, engineering, marketing, and partnerships.

### 1. For anyone and everyone

- No artificial restrictions that shut people out of opportunity.
- No shaming, ranking people into “losers,” or designs that exist to make users feel small.
- This product is aimed at **helping people up**, not suppressing them or putting them down.

### 2. Learning is part of the product

Learning in all aspects of life is **just as important to your business** as learning the data. We encourage growth as whole people — discipline, relationships, integrity, health — not only dashboard metrics.

### 3. Honest, upright, never predatory

- We will be **honest and upright** — not deceptive, not dishonest.
- We will **never take advantage of someone in a poor situation** (financial distress, low literacy with tools, crisis, desperation).
- Dark patterns, hidden fees, fake scarcity, misleading “AI certainty,” and exploitative upsells are **out of scope**.

### 4. Philanthropic by intent

We will be **philanthropic** where we can — helping people who might not otherwise have the opportunity to join what we are building (access programs, free tiers, education, community support). Commercial sustainability and generosity must coexist, not cancel each other out.

## Red flag protocol

**If any line of code, feature, copy, business rule, or experiment would violate the above:**

1. **Stop** — do not merge quietly and hope no one notices.
2. **Name the conflict** — which principle is at risk, and how.
3. **Raise a red flag** — discuss with Steven or the team before proceeding.
4. **Propose an alternative** that still serves users and the business without crossing the line.

Examples that should trigger review:

- Paywalls that trap users who cannot afford to leave bad advice behind  
- Copy that humiliates beginners or exaggerates guaranteed income  
- Data presentation that hides uncertainty to push a sale  
- Features that benefit LarisID by harming sellers  
- Excluding groups of people without a clear, ethical, user-first reason  

When in doubt, **choose the path that protects the person with less power in the interaction** — usually the seller, not the platform.
