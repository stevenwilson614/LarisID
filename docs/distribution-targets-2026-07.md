**Priority: Katadata/Databoks.** They are a data-journalism outlet that publishes charts and
already maintains a social-commerce tag. The 100-city dataset is literally their format.
Send this one first and properly rather than blasting all ten.

**Realistic count.** There are roughly 10–15 genuinely relevant outlets in this market, not
100. Ten individually-researched pitches referencing a specific reporter's recent work will
outperform a hundred generic ones — journalists spot a blast instantly, and bulk unsolicited
commercial mail carries real legal exposure plus the domain-reputation cost above.

### Two things to know before posting

1. **"Seller Online Shop (Sharing dan Diskusi)" hides a code in its rules.** Rule 3 contains
   `kode rahasia: sos`. Admins use this to verify you read the rules; posts that omit it may
   be rejected. Its rules otherwise *explicitly welcome* our content — "hanya untuk sharing
   diskusi dan tips trik cara jualan online" — but rule 1 is "Dilarang Jualan Apapun."
2. **That group already has 1 post + 1 comment pending admin approval** on this account.
   Worth checking what those are before adding more — if something promotional is sitting in
   the queue, a second post could read as pressure.

### Strategy: earn standing first, no links

Per the decision to leave LarisID unmentioned for now: post genuinely useful data, no link,
no brand. **Be clear-eyed that this has zero direct SEO or AI-referral value** — a post with
no link and no brand mention passes no equity and creates no citation. Its only value is
establishing the account as a real contributor so that a *disclosed* mention later is
credible rather than drive-by. That is a legitimate play, but it is groundwork, not
distribution.

Rules that keep it honest and keep the account safe:
- Never mention LarisID while posing as a neutral party. When we do mention it, disclose
  ("disclosure: gue yang bikin").
- One post per group per week maximum. Same text across several groups the same day is the
  classic spam signal.
- Reply to comments. A post that gets engagement and no author replies looks automated.
- Always carry the estimasi caveat, same as on-site.

## Draft post — no brand, no link (Indonesian)

For **Komunitas Seller Shopee Indonesia** (primary target).

> **Data: 8 kategori yang permintaannya paling terbukti buat penjual pemula (2026)**
>
> Gue sempat ngumpulin data listing Shopee buat cari tahu kategori mana yang permintaannya
> benar-benar terbukti — bukan sekadar yang lagi viral. Ini hasilnya, semoga berguna:
>
> Kategori dengan bukti permintaan paling kuat, semuanya modal di bawah Rp 50.000/unit:
> skincare, makanan hewan, aksesoris fashion (jam tangan), aksesoris HP (kabel data),
> kerajinan/hobi, perlengkapan pesta, alat tulis sekolah, dan pertanian/berkebun.
>
> Tapi ini bagian yang menurut gue paling penting, dan sering kebalik:
>
> **Jangan tiru produk terlarisnya.** Produk yang ulasannya ratusan ribu itu justru yang
> paling mustahil kamu lawan sebagai pemula. Angka itu gunanya cuma satu: bukti bahwa
> KATEGORI-nya punya permintaan. Masuknya lewat niche yang lebih sempit di dalam kategori itu.
>
> Satu pola lain dari datanya: produk yang akhirnya laku median-nya ada di sekitar 64 ulasan,
> sementara yang gagal median-nya 1 ulasan. Jadi hambatan terbesar penjual baru itu bukan
> produk atau harga — tapi nembus ~50 ulasan pertama. Kalau lagi di fase itu dan rasanya
> stuck, itu normal, dan itu memang bagian tersulitnya.
>
> Catatan biar fair: harga, rating, dan jumlah ulasan itu data listing nyata. Angka "terjual"
> dan omzet itu ESTIMASI dari data publik, bukan angka resmi Shopee.
>
> Ada yang punya pengalaman beda soal 50 ulasan pertama? Penasaran gimana cara kalian nembusnya.

**Why this shape:** it gives away the actual finding rather than teasing it, admits its own
data limitation, and ends with a real question — which is what generates comments, and
comments are what make the next post from this account visible.
