# Data pitch — Indonesian e-commerce media (Juli 2026)

Goal: earn independent third-party citations of LarisID's proprietary dataset. This is the
lever that neither schema markup nor llms.txt can substitute for — both Google's link graph
and LLM citation layers weight *other people writing about you*.

Pitch the **data**, not the product. Journalists ignore product pitches and respond to
exclusive numbers. The product link comes along for free in the attribution.

---

## The assets we actually have (verified against the live site)

- **745** keyword-level Shopee market pages, public, no login
- **100** city/regency pages across Java — this is the genuinely unique one
- Example (Surabaya): **3,123 active Shopee sellers**, **8,489 products** monitored over 45
  days, **median price Rp 29.900**
- Per-niche beginner breakout odds, and the review-wall finding (median winner ≈64 reviews,
  median flop = 1)

Caveat that must survive into any coverage: **harga / rating / jumlah ulasan are real listing
data; "terjual" and omzet are ESTIMATES.** Say this in the pitch itself — journalists trust
sources that volunteer their own limitations, and it protects us if a number is quoted.

---

## Angle ranking (strongest first)

**1. Peta ekonomi penjual Shopee per kota di Jawa.**
Nobody publishes city-level marketplace seller data in Indonesia. Regional-economy desks and
data-journalism outlets can build a map or chart straight from it. Strongest and most
exclusive.

**2. "Dinding ulasan": kenapa mayoritas penjual baru gagal di 50 ulasan pertama.**
Median winner ≈64 reviews, median flop = 1. Counterintuitive, concrete, quotable, and it is
a genuinely useful public-interest finding for the millions of UMKM trying to sell online.

**3. Produk terlaris untuk pemula 2026, dengan bukti.**
Ties to the published guide. Weakest as news (every outlet runs listicles) but the easiest
yes, and good for lifestyle/UMKM desks.

## Rules for this outreach

- Send **individually**. Never CC or BCC a list.
- Lead with the data, never with the product.
- **Always** include the estimasi caveat. It costs nothing and buys credibility.
- Offer a custom cut (CSV/chart) — that is what converts a "maybe" into a story.
- One follow-up after ~7 days, then stop.
- Never pay for coverage or offer anything in exchange for a link.
